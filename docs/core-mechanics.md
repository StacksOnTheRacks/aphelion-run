# Core mechanics

Mine a body, store the haul, spend it on improvements and repairs. Energy from the Sun and from home pays for travel and for the cloak. Players mine minerals. Energy taken from the Sun is not a mineral. Minerals and energy share one cargo unit. One of either takes one space in the hold. Solscrip takes none. Energy is sold for Solscrip at Earth's black market.

| Mechanic | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | Stores the energy and minerals a player has collected. Equipment is static: the gravity boom, the warehouse, the repair bay, the dock, and the water store. Each upgrades to level 100. The base advances when every piece reaches the next step of ten. | Recorded |
| Mining | A player gathers a mineral by going to the body that has it, including the home body. | Recorded |
| Upgrades | Minerals improve and repair a ship. They do not buy a new hull. Banked Solscrip can finish an improvement sooner. It does not replace a missing mineral, and it does not shorten a repair. | Recorded |
| Ship class | Miner, Warship, Explorer, and Hauler. Each class has five variants, read on storage capacity, siphon speed, weapon strength, speed adjusted by weight, and energy burn rate. Hull strength is separate from the weapons. A ship carries one siphon, one thruster, one drill, and one cloak, each replaceable and upgradable to level 10. Names and numbers are not locked. | Recorded |
| Ships | The player starts with one ship. Another ship is bought with Solscrip at Earth's black market. | Recorded |
| Travel | Moving a ship spends energy, in the same cargo units as a mineral. Inner orbit is a slow impulse rocket. Between bodies, a photon rocket. Alignment and a gravity assist still change the cost. The player always sees the trip and the return before departure. | Recorded |
| Wormhole | Each system has one at its edge. A ship leaves through its own and arrives through the target's. Other systems cannot be entered yet. What the hop costs is not set. | Recorded |
| Cloak | Cloaking spends energy. A cloaked player cannot be attacked. | Recorded |
| Outer orbit | A ship in an outer orbit can challenge a ship that is mining that body, or a ship sitting idle in that outer orbit. A depleted mine or a finished Earth sale puts the ship back in that idle orbit. A ship on a photon burn cannot be attacked. The Moon is not a combat zone. | Recorded |
| Solar energy | Energy is taken from satellites the unnamed company owns. Siphoning one is theft, as that company sees it. | Recorded |
| Gravity boom | A boom on the home works the pull along its length as the base orbits, and feeds a slow stream of energy into the warehouse. Every home gets the same rate. | Recorded |
| Sell at Earth | A player hauls excess energy to Earth's black market and sells it. The pay is Solscrip, banked at home. It can finish an improvement sooner, and it can buy another ship. Whether a completed sale moves the price is not decided. | Recorded |

## Gravity boom

A boom on the home works the pull along its length as the base orbits, and feeds a slow stream of energy into the warehouse. Every home gets the same rate, so a home near the Sun is not richer. The stream can cover a departure if the pilot waits. It does not replace a haul from the Sun.

## Outer orbit

An earlier note called this the lower orbit, and said a player traveling outside it cannot be attacked. That is the same rule as the brief: the outer orbit is the battle surface, and a photon burn between bodies is not. The Moon stays the exception.
