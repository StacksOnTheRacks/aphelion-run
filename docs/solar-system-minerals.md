# Minerals by planet

Players mine four minerals: hydrogen, ore, nitrogen, and carbon. Ships are improved and repaired from all four. A new hull is bought with Solscrip. Energy taken from the Sun is not a mineral. Water is not a mineral. Ice is mined as hydrogen, and the oxygen in that ice is not tracked. Oxygen rides in the ore.

A world is a planet and its moons. They share one yield. A moon’s yield is counted on its planet, so a base on a moon has that planet’s minerals.

Water ice is counted as hydrogen. Methane is counted as carbon. A rocky core under a deep atmosphere is not counted as ore.

The Sun yields energy. Earth yields nothing a ship can mine. The Moon shares Earth’s yield, so it yields nothing either, and it can still hold a home.
