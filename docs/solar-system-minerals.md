# Minerals by planet

Players mine minerals. The minerals are hydrogen, ore, nitrogen, carbon, and water. Ships are improved and repaired from hydrogen, ore, nitrogen, and carbon. A new hull is bought with Solscrip. Water is a mineral too, and the home base consumes it. Energy taken from the Sun is not a mineral.

A world is a planet and its moons. They share one yield. A moon’s yield is counted on its planet, so a base on a moon has that planet’s minerals.

Hydrogen bound in water is counted as water. Methane is counted as carbon. A rocky core under a deep atmosphere is not counted as ore.

The Sun yields energy. Earth yields nothing a ship can mine. The Moon shares Earth’s yield, so it yields nothing either, and it can still hold a home.
